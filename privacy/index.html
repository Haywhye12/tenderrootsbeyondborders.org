Privacy & Cookie Policy

How Tender Roots Beyond Borders Inc. safeguards your personal data, donor information, and online privacy.

Effective Date: August 26, 2026 | Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction & Commitment

Tender Roots Beyond Borders Inc. (“TRBB”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) is an incorporated non-profit organization approved by the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) as a tax-exempt organization under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code. We are deeply committed to maintaining the trust and confidence of our donors, volunteers, partners, and website visitors. This Privacy & Cookie Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and disclose information when you visit our website or interact with our programs across the United States, Nigeria, and Malawi.

2. Donor Privacy Protection

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share donor personal information with any third-party commercial entities under any circumstances. Donor contact details (such as name, email address, mailing address, and phone number) are strictly used for issuing tax receipts, acknowledging contributions, and providing updates regarding our humanitarian missions.

3. Secure Payment Gateway (Flutterwave)

All online financial transactions and donations processed on our website are securely handled by Flutterwave Inc., a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified global payment gateway. TRBB does not collect, store, or process your credit card numbers, bank pin details, or mobile money security credentials on our local servers. Payment processing is end-to-end encrypted using 256-bit SSL technology in accordance with international banking security standards.

4. Cookie Policy

Cookies are small text files stored on your browser or device when you visit our website. We use cookies and similar browser storage technologies (`localStorage`) to enhance user experience:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for fundamental site security, page navigation, and smooth modal overlays (including donation triggers).
  • Functional Preferences: Remembers your chosen donation currency (USD, NGN, MWK, GBP, EUR) and cookie consent status so you don’t have to re-enter them on repeat visits.
  • Performance & Security: Helps protect against spam and bot attacks on our contact forms and donation modals.

5. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:

  • Submit an inquiry via our Contact Form (Name, Email, Phone, Message subject).
  • Make a tax-deductible donation via our Flutterwave Gateway (Name, Email, Payment amount, Target program).
  • Sign up for volunteer programs, educational sponsorships, or career mentorship networks.

6. Data Rights & Contact Information

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections, or request deletion of your information from our mailing list at any time. If you have questions regarding this Privacy & Cookie Policy, please contact us:

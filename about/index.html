A Few Words

Hope to Sprout Again

Tender Roots Beyond Borders Inc., is a nonprofit organization that is incorporated and approved by the IRS as tax-exempt organization in the United States of America.

We are committed to the cause of creating opportunities for new hope to thrive for neglected individuals who are overwhelmed by daunting challenges of life that have put them in a state of despair and plunged them into deep despondency. The divine inspiration that generates the sense of possibilities that drives the mission of TRBB is derived from the experiences of the visionary leader whose life received the hope to sprout again through the word of God from a seemingly hopeless situation.

“For there is hope for a tree, if it be cut down, that it will sprout again, and that its tender shoots will not cease.” — Job 14:7-9

What We See

Our Vision Statement

To become a preferred haven providing refuge, empowerment, and holistic restoration.

Vision Statement

“To become a preferred haven, which provides a refuge of hope and empowerment facilities for the hurting and socio-economically deprived individuals to heal and blossom again in life.”

Our Inspiration Slogan: The Hope to Sprout Again (Job 14:7-9)

Mission Statement

To provide transitional room and board, therapy, social amenities, healthcare, supplies for educational and daily needs with empowerment programs for the less-privileged, orphans and the socially challenged, thereby inspiring the hope for them to sprout again in life.

Value Statement

To invest part of the proceeds of our business into the provisions for the total well-being of our neighbors as the pillar for a thriving society, through value-based intervention programs that are built on the foundation of the fear of God, justice, and equal opportunities for all.

Our History

Milestones Beyond Borders

From divine revelation to global humanitarian impact.

2018

Divine Inception

Job 14:7-9 scripture was impressed upon Pastor Aderoju Ajibade's spirit to establish Tender Roots for vulnerable women, orphans, and youths.

2020

IRS Tax-Exempt Status

Incorporated and officially approved by the IRS as a tax-exempt non-profit organization in the United States of America.

2022

Nigeria Cities Expansion

Established active outreach operations in four major cities across Nigeria: Ogbomosho, Ibadan, Benin City, and Lagos State.

2024

Malawi International Mission

Extended international outreach to Blantyre, Malawi, partnering with Maoni Orphanage Home to provide room, board, and education.

IRS Approved Non-Profit

Building a Legacy of Hope Across Continents

Approved 501(c)(3) status delivering sustainable empowerment, healthcare, and education.

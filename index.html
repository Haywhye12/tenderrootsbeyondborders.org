A Few Words About Us

Restoring Hope & Dignity

Tender Roots Beyond Borders Inc., is a nonprofit organization incorporated and approved by the IRS as tax-exempt organization in the United States of America.

We are committed to creating opportunities for new hope to thrive for neglected individuals who are overwhelmed by daunting challenges of life that have put them in a state of despair.

Strategic Pillars

Our Core Programs

Empowering communities through socio-economic intervention, skills training, and respite housing.

Intervention Pillar

New Hope To-Blossom

Socio-economic intervention program catering to vulnerable youths and adults growing in an environment of insecurity and uncertainty.

Empowerment Pillar

Sprouting To-Thrive

Empowerment and wellbeing program encompassing vocational training, girl-child education, healthcare, therapy, and nutrition education.

Shelter & Respite

Haven of New Hope

Transitional room and board, respite, and professional counseling for hurting and homeless individuals to heal and blossom again.

Global Impact Missions

Restoring Dignity to Overwhelmed Lives

From orphanages in Blantyre, Malawi to care missions in Ogbomoso, Ibadan, Benin, Abuja, Ogijo, and Lagos.
